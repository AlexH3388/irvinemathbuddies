Our mission

Why we started

Our goal is to make math enjoyable, build confidence, and help every child reach their potential.

01

Tutoring in Alderwood

Establish a program in Alderwood where elementary students receive tutoring from high school volunteers.

02

Bridge the language gap

Help families where language is a barrier, so parents and children can both stay connected to schoolwork.

03

Make math fun

We teach kids interactively, with games and activities, instead of long lectures.

What makes Math Buddies special

What we offer

Personalized support

One-on-one and small-group math help in a welcoming environment for all students.

Games & activities

Fun, hands-on math games that keep kids interested while they learn.

Homework help

Homework assistance and extra practice opportunities to keep students on track.

Mentors who get it

High-achieving high schoolers with recent experience in elementary math topics who encourage and inspire.

Multilingual volunteers

Support in English, Farsi, Chinese, and more, for students and their families.

A community that learns together

Math help that speaks your family's language

We understand that some families face language barriers when helping their children with schoolwork. Our multilingual volunteers can help bridge that gap and create a more supportive learning experience at school and at home.

Interested in joining?

Two ways to be a buddy

Together, we can make math more accessible, enjoyable, and empowering for every child.

Elementary families

Register your child for Math Buddies. Start with our short interest form: tell us about your child, your neighborhood, and which days work best for your family.

  • Open to Alderwood students in grades 1–6
  • Homework help, extra practice, and enrichment
  • Volunteers who can talk with you in your language
Open the interest form

High school students

Earn community service hours while making a real impact. Fill out the short volunteer form with your availability, and we'll reach out. Be someone's favorite math mentor!

  • Help younger students build math confidence
  • Teach through games, puzzles, and creative activities
  • Share your language skills (multilingual volunteers especially welcome)
  • Build leadership, communication, and mentoring skills
  • Earn valuable community service hours
Volunteer interest form

You don't have to be a math genius. You just need patience, enthusiasm, and a willingness to help others learn!
